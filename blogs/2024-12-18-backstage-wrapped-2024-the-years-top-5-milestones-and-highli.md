<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="5️⃣-60k-contributions-from-a-global-community-of-very-busy-beavers">5️⃣: 60k+ Contributions from a Global Community of Very Busy Beavers🦫<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#5%EF%B8%8F%E2%83%A3-60k-contributions-from-a-global-community-of-very-busy-beavers" title="Direct link to 5️⃣: 60k+ Contributions from a Global Community of Very Busy Beavers🦫">​</a></h2>

<p><em>Behold! Every commit to the Backstage open source project this year — can you
see your name in there?</em></p>
<p>As we shared last month when we kicked off <a class="" href="https://youtu.be/OfavM6j4Z5o?list=PL8iP9yIjU0Q3UUDXdAoCaTC4Ci8asWg-X&amp;t=112" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">BackstageCon North America</a>, the Backstage community hit a new milestone this year — reaching 60,000 total contributions (discussions, issues, and pull requests) to the project since Spotify donated it to the <a class="" href="https://www.cncf.io/projects/backstage/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF)</a> in 2020. Busy Backstage beavers, indeed.</p>
<p>And the community was as active as ever in 2024 — if you hit play on the visualization above, you can see all 12.3k commits from the year being made. (Though, instead of busy beavers, it's more like a giant cluster of space spiders shooting galactic laser webs. Pew pew!)</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="the-most-commits-of-any-cncf-project-by-a-lot">The most commits of any CNCF project, by a lot<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#the-most-commits-of-any-cncf-project-by-a-lot" title="Direct link to The most commits of any CNCF project, by a lot">​</a></h3>
<p><img alt="Taylor Dolezal presenting a chart showing the Top 10 CNCF Projects by Number of End User Commits on the Keynote stage at KubeCon Europe 2024" class="img_ev3q" height="844" src="https://backstage.io/assets/images/cncf-keynote-kubecon-2024-6b5b54b50be25b9639a795634436f65f.jpg" width="1500" /></p>
<p>Speaking of commits, rewinding back to <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/03/27/backstagecon-kubecon-24/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe</a> in March gives us another way to visualize the mightiness of the Backstage community.</p>
<p>From the keynote stage in Paris, Taylor Dolezal, the head of ecosystem at The Linux Foundation, <a class="" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MICHGBAe8gc&amp;list=PLj6h78yzYM2N8nw1YcqqKveySH6_0VnI0&amp;index=161" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">presented a chart</a> showing the "Top CNCF Projects by Number of End User Commits", using data from the previous four months.</p>
<p>At the top of the chart, a conspicuous blue bar — more than twice the length as the next longest one — had Taylor noting that, among CNCF projects, Backstage was "rocketing into the forefront when it comes to end user contributions".🚀🚀🚀</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="our-first-contribfest">Our first Contribfest!<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#our-first-contribfest" title="Direct link to Our first Contribfest!">​</a></h3>
<p><img alt="A room full of developers working together at the first Backstage Contribfest" class="img_ev3q" height="900" src="https://backstage.io/assets/images/wrapped2024-contribfest-84bc9ce9d69a641a96914ad3b3961834.jpg" width="1200" /></p>
<p>What's better than ogling a nifty visualization or nerding out on a good bar graph? Feeling the buzz of those contributions in person.</p>
<p>Just last month at KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America, maintainers from Spotify and Red Hat cohosted the first-ever <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/09/kubecon-slc-24/#contribfest-its-all-about-community" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Backstage Contribfest</a>. We packed the room with engineers obsessed with developer experience and let them loose on a curated list of issues. The session was good, old-fashioned coding fun — devs sharing ideas and solving problems together — and we look forward to doing it again with all of you.</p>
<p>If you missed it and want to get in on the action, just follow along with the <a class="" href="https://static.sched.com/hosted_files/kccncna2024/53/KubeCon-ContribFest-Backstage-NA-2024.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Contribfest slide deck</a>. Or meet us in 💂‍♂️London in April for <a class="" href="https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-europe/co-located-events/backstagecon/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">BackstageCon</a> and <a class="" href="https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-europe/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2025</a>!</p>

<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="4️⃣-210-open-source-plugins-and-counting">4️⃣: 210+ Open Source Plugins (and Counting)🧩<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#4%EF%B8%8F%E2%83%A3-210-open-source-plugins-and-counting" title="Direct link to 4️⃣: 210+ Open Source Plugins (and Counting)🧩">​</a></h2>
<p><img alt="Bowie the Backstage Beaver surrounded by icons for plugins: Argo CD, Datadog, GitHub Actions, Grafana, Jira, and Kubernetes" class="img_ev3q" height="675" src="https://backstage.io/assets/images/wrapped2024-4-plugins-d27ba2857ca81e3e2ca87ab732fad606.gif" width="1200" />
<em>Just a few of the plugins built by the Backstage community</em></p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="plugins-plugins-plugins">Plugins, plugins, plugins<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#plugins-plugins-plugins" title="Direct link to Plugins, plugins, plugins">​</a></h3>
<p>Everybody's building them, everywhere you look. We see official plugins from popular third-party service providers (<a class="" href="https://backstage.spotify.com/marketplace/dx/plugin/dx/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">DX</a>, <a class="" href="https://pagerduty.github.io/backstage-plugin-docs/index.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">PagerDuty</a>, <a class="" href="https://backstage.spotify.com/marketplace/rootly/plugin/rootly/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Rootly</a>), in-house plugins going open source (like these from <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community-plugins/blob/main/workspaces/github-pull-requests-board/plugins/github-pull-requests-board/README.md" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">DAZN</a>, <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community-plugins/blob/main/workspaces/azure-devops/plugins/azure-devops/README.md" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Keyloop</a>, and <a class="" href="https://github.com/seatgeek/backstage-plugins" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">SeatGeek</a>), essential plugins built and maintained by ecosystem champions and supporters (<a class="" href="https://github.com/awslabs/backstage-plugins-for-aws" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">AWS</a>, <a class="" href="https://github.com/googlecloudplatform/google-cloud-backstage-plugins" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">GCP</a>, <a class="" href="https://roadie.io/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Roadie</a>, <a class="" href="https://backstage.spotify.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Spotify</a>), as well as creative and <a class="" href="https://github.com/procore-oss/backstage-plugin-announcements" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">useful</a> plugins from contributors and enthusiasts from all corners of the Backstage community.</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="a-yearlong-plugin-party">A yearlong plugin party<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#a-yearlong-plugin-party" title="Direct link to A yearlong plugin party">​</a></h3>
<p>In 2024, the plugin ecosystem continued to expand — with about a hundred new plugins being introduced over the year and many others getting big updates.</p>
<p>SeatGeek started the plugin party in February❄️ at a Backstage Community Session where they <a class="" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CRw0m3J11Bc&amp;t=526s" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">demoed</a> five of the seven plugins they've open sourced, including their <a class="" href="https://github.com/seatgeek/backstage-plugins/tree/main/plugins/awards" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Awards Plugin</a> and <a class="" href="https://github.com/seatgeek/backstage-plugins/tree/main/plugins/entity-scaffolder-content" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Entity Scaffolder Plugin</a>.</p>
<p>By spring🌷, new plugins for <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community-plugins/tree/main/workspaces/linkerd" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Linkerd</a> and <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community-plugins/tree/main/workspaces/blackduck/plugins/blackduck" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Black Duck</a> had sprung onto the scene.</p>
<p>During the summer☀️, the official <a class="" href="https://pagerduty.github.io/backstage-plugin-docs/index.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">PagerDuty</a> plugin received updates that made it a breeze to configure existing PagerDuty services and also added support for multiple PagerDuty accounts in the same Backstage instance.</p>
<p>By fall🍁, some of the most-used plugins — including plugins for <a class="" href="https://www.npmjs.com/package/@roadiehq/backstage-plugin-argo-cd" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Argo CD</a>, <a class="" href="https://www.npmjs.com/package/@roadiehq/backstage-plugin-datadog" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Datadog</a>, <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community-plugins/tree/main/workspaces/github-actions/plugins/github-actions" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">GitHub Actions</a>, <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community-plugins/blob/main/workspaces/grafana/plugins/grafana/docs/index.md" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Grafana</a>, <a class="" href="https://github.com/AxisCommunications/backstage-plugins/tree/main/plugins/jira-dashboard" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Jira</a>, <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/features/kubernetes/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Kubernetes</a>, and <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community-plugins/tree/main/workspaces/tech-radar/plugins/tech-radar" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Tech Radar</a> — had already been updated to support the very latest versions of the Backstage framework's <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/releases/v1.30.0" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">New Frontend System</a> and <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/backstage/issues/24493" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">New Backend System</a>. (Thanks to the teams at Roadie and Spotify for helping to kickstart a new era of easy-to-install-and-configure plugins!)</p>
<p>And last but not least, AWS just released the <a class="" href="https://github.com/awslabs/backstage-plugins-for-aws/blob/main/plugins/core/catalog-config/README.md" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">AWS Config catalog plugin for Backstage</a> — a great new plugin that makes it easy for their customers to ingest AWS resources into the Backstage Software Catalog.</p>
<p>Have a plugin you'd like to show off and share with the community? Sign up to demo it at one of our monthly <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community/tree/main/backstage-community-sessions#readme" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Backstage Community Sessions</a>!</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="a-shiny-new-plugins-repo">A shiny new plugins repo<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#a-shiny-new-plugins-repo" title="Direct link to A shiny new plugins repo">​</a></h3>
<p>Alongside all this plugin-building action, there was also plenty of plugin-migrating action. In April, community plugins moved to a repo they could call their own — the <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/04/19/community-plugins/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Backstage Community Plugins Repository</a>.</p>
<p>With release tooling, workspace tooling, and migration guide all included, <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community-plugins" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">the new repo</a> gives plugin owners a leg up on building and shipping plugins. Now you can release independently of the main monorepo, at your own release cadence.</p>
<p>Special thanks go to Red Hat as maintainers of the <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/backstage/blob/master/OWNERS.md#community-plugins" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">community plugins project area</a> and to everyone who provided feedback on the migration plan. With the new community plugins repo now established, it will be fascinating to see what new, innovative plugins we'll be talking about this time next year. <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/plugins/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Go start building</a>!</p>

<p><img alt="Bowie the Backstage Beaver wearing glasses and holding a book pointing at the badge for Certified Backstage Associate" class="img_ev3q" height="675" src="https://backstage.io/assets/images/wrapped2024-2-cba-944a4303d0c9b9a990789cc1598f65a3.gif" width="1200" />
<em>Time to make it official</em></p>
<p>With the Backstage community growing across so many companies, the need for Backstage experts grows, as well. Now there's an easy way to spot 'em.</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="become-a-certified-backstage-associate-cba">Become a Certified Backstage Associate (CBA)<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#become-a-certified-backstage-associate-cba" title="Direct link to Become a Certified Backstage Associate (CBA)">​</a></h3>
<p>Last month, the CNCF and Linux Foundation Education <a class="" href="https://training.linuxfoundation.org/blog/just-launched-certified-backstage-associate-cba/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">announced</a> the launch of a new certification: <a class="" href="https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/certified-backstage-associate-cba/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Certified Backstage Associate (CBA)</a>. So now you can make your expertise for "building on top of the most popular open source IDP" official:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Designed for IT engineers, developers, platform engineers, and other IT professionals, the CBA proves you have the mindset and skills to work in and build Backstage tools that improve performance and drive innovation benefiting your career, your team and your organization.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>It's a huge help for Backstage experts and practitioners, as well as all those Backstage adopters (and future adopters) who prioritize developer experience and productivity amid the increasing complexity of modern software development.</p>
<p>As Pia Nilsson, head of platform developer experience at Spotify, says in the announcement, "With the CNCF's Certified Backstage Associate program, companies now have a way to identify and tap into that expertise, while helping to contribute back to the growing Backstage community."</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="enroll-today">Enroll today!<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#enroll-today" title="Direct link to Enroll today!">​</a></h3>
<p>The program to become a Certified Backstage Associate is live, so you can <a class="" href="https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/certified-backstage-associate-cba/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">enroll today</a>!</p>
<p>And if you want to see what domains the 90-minute, multiple-choice CBA exam covers — and hear more about how "earning a Backstage certification can be a career game-changer for developers and IT engineer's careers" — makes sure to read the full announcement on the <a class="" href="https://training.linuxfoundation.org/blog/just-launched-certified-backstage-associate-cba/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">The Linux Foundation Education blog</a>.</p>

<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="1️⃣-version-10-of-the-future-of-backstage">1️⃣: Version 1.0 of the Future of Backstage🎉<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#1%EF%B8%8F%E2%83%A3-version-10-of-the-future-of-backstage" title="Direct link to 1️⃣: Version 1.0 of the Future of Backstage🎉">​</a></h2>
<p><img alt="The top half of Bowie the Backstage Beaver sticking out of the top of a portal, the beaver&amp;#39;s bottom half sticking out of the bottom of another portal, with Backend System 1.0! written in between the floating halves" class="img_ev3q" height="675" src="https://backstage.io/assets/images/wrapped2024-1-backend-ce46f7bf4b40d1ce9232e188bb951993.gif" width="1200" />
<em>And the number one milestone of 2024 is…Backend System 1.0!</em></p>
<p>As we saw in the video visualizing a year's worth of commits, there were a lot of changes to Backstage in 2024 — including improved documentation, the introduction of the Framework SIG (<a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community/tree/main/sigs#sig" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">join a Backstage special interest group</a>!), Yarn 4 as the default, our second security audit, a built-in event bus, major new features to the Scaffolder UI (form decorators and retries — shout-out to <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/backstage/blob/master/OWNERS.md#scaffolder" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Bogdan Nechyporenko</a> from Bol.com!), big strides in the plugin-building experience in the <a class="" href="https://youtu.be/BzPCJMQH8tg?list=PL8iP9yIjU0Q3UUDXdAoCaTC4Ci8asWg-X" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">New Frontend System</a> — and much, much more. And that was just in the last six months.</p>
<p>But the 1.0 release of the New Backend System was the biggie of the year.</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="the-new-backend-system-becomes-the-backend-system">The "New Backend System" becomes <em>the</em> backend system<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#the-new-backend-system-becomes-the-backend-system" title="Direct link to the-new-backend-system-becomes-the-backend-system">​</a></h3>
<p>After months of testing, feedback, and improvements with the community, the New Backend System landed as a <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/releases/v1.31.0/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">stable v.1.0 release</a> in September — marking the beginning of a new era for building for Backstage.</p>
<p>The New Backend System (which shall henceforth be known as "<em>the</em> backend system") brings a host of improvements to the way that backends and their features are constructed. Instead of the old convention-based structure that could lead to migration pains, features now fit together with dependency injection, like a jigsaw puzzle, while leveraging powerful core services and extending each other dynamically with additional behaviors.</p>
<p>And this is just scratching the surface of it. To see what else is new in the backend system — check out <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/backend-system/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">the docs</a>!</p>
<p>We are very excited about this important milestone after a long period of development and refinement together with our passionate community, and hope that you will find it as refreshing and simple yet powerful as it was envisioned to be. This also lays the foundation for us being able to continue iterating at a high pace without incurring undue end user breakages during upgrades.</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="how-to-migrate-your-backends-and-plugins">How to migrate your backends and plugins<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2024/12/18/backstage-wrapped-2024#how-to-migrate-your-backends-and-plugins" title="Direct link to How to migrate your backends and plugins">​</a></h3>
<p>We have documentation for both <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/backend-system/building-backends/migrating" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">migrating backends</a> and <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/backend-system/building-plugins-and-modules/migrating" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">migrating plugins</a>. If you haven't migrated already, we encourage you to do so as soon as possible, so that all adopters and end users can begin to unlock the benefits of the new system!</p>
<p>And keep the feedback coming, either on the community <a class="" href="https://discord.gg/backstage-687207715902193673" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Discord</a> or through <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/backstage/issues/new/choose" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">issues</a> on GitHub. If you're looking for a way to help, take a look at these open issues in the <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/backstage/issues/26353" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">backstage</a> and <a class="" href="https://github.com/backstage/community-plugins/issues/1176" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">community plugins</a> repos about deprecating old backend capabilities of already migrated plugins. To the future we go!</p>
