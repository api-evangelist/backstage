<p>Earlier this year, the Backstage project <a class="" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JqG1wey7-Ao&amp;t=563s&amp;list=PL8iP9yIjU0Q3K8LV_a9CcFmhvvNTAUzL1" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">celebrated its 5th birthday</a> on the main stage at <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2025/04/29/backstagecon-kubecon-25-london#big-birthday-energy-on-the-big-stage" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">KubeCon + CloudNativeCon in London</a>.</p>
<p>While the framework still isn't old enough to shave, it's matured into the industry standard for internal developer portals (IDPs). According to <a class="" href="https://newsletter.getdx.com/p/backstage-and-the-developer-portal-market" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">this DX survey</a> released in March:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Backstage is dominating the IDP market, holding an impressive 89% market share compared to SaaS competitors and achieving a remarkable 67% overall market penetration. These percentages are even higher for enterprise companies.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>As you can see above in the <a class="" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wih0a1v1Et0&amp;list=PL8iP9yIjU0Q33vpSaBlAvIhgDb-9smXUU" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">latest project stats</a>, the Backstage ecosystem continues to expand, growing in adopters and contributors, as well as plugins and third-party service providers.</p>
<p>Want to dive into more Backstage stats? Check out the project's health scores and more on the relaunched <a class="" href="https://insights.linuxfoundation.org/project/backstage" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">LFX Insights</a> site.</p>
<h2 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_Vzrq" id="building-the-future-on-modern-foundations">Building the future on modern foundations<a class="hash-link" href="https://backstage.io/blog/2025/12/30/backstage-wrapped-2025#building-the-future-on-modern-foundations" title="Direct link to Building the future on modern foundations">​</a></h2>
<p>Just because Backstage has cemented its place as the standard for IDPs doesn't mean it's sitting still. From backend to frontend, from AI to UI, the technical foundations of the platform have been modernized, updated, and revamped — this is why today's Backstage is ready to build the future of developer experience.</p>
<p>Top 2025 highlights:</p>
<ul>
<li class="">Goodbye to the old backend system</li>
<li class="">New Frontend System is adoption-ready</li>
<li class="">Hello to the Actions Registry + MCP server support</li>
<li class="">Introducing a new design system with Backstage UI</li>
</ul>
<p>With the <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/backend-system/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">New Backend System</a> released as a stable 1.0 last year and the <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/frontend-system/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">New Frontend System</a> becoming <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/releases/v1.42.0" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">adoption-ready</a> this year, Backstage is easier than ever to use, maintain, and build on — making it both more accessible and more capable for adopters, old and new.</p>
<p>This year also saw the debut of the <a class="" href="https://backstage.io/docs/backend-system/core-services/actions-registry" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Actions Registry</a> and MCP server support — so now Backstage can empower both your human developers and their AI helpers.</p>
<p>And <a class="" href="https://ui.backstage.io/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Backstage UI</a> is coming! The alpha release of the upcoming design system made huge strides in 2025, preparing the way for a new level of usability throughout the Backstage experience.</p>
